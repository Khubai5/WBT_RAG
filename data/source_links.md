Ingestion notes
1. Start with the official website pages and the uploaded WBT_RAG_Knowledge_Base.pdf.
2. Capture page title, canonical URL, retrieval date, publication or effective date when available, region, source type, and confidence/status.
3. Prefer current, first-party approved sources for current offers and policies. Mark undated or older material as historical or unverified until confirmed.
4. Preserve exact prices, currencies, qualifiers, and geographic scope. Surface conflicts instead of merging them.
5. Answers should cite the source title and URL, and should say when the collected sources do not establish an answer.
6. Do not ingest secrets, personal data, or restricted community content without authorization.
